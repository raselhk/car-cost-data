# Sources

Every figure in these datasets comes from the sources below, as cited in the original CarCostWise articles.

## Car sales tax by state (`car-sales-tax-by-state-2026.csv`)

- Florida Department of Revenue, TIP 25A01-01R, "Motor Vehicle Sales Tax Rates by State" (rates and trade-in rules for each state, revised January 31, 2025)
- Tax Foundation, "State and Local Sales Tax Rates, Midyear 2026" (July 6, 2026)
- California Department of Tax and Fee Administration, "Applying Tax to Your Sales and Purchases" (trade-ins not deductible)
- Virginia DMV, Sales and Use Tax (4.15%, $75 minimum, no trade-in credit)
- DC DMV, Vehicle Title (excise tax by weight and fuel economy, effective February 17, 2025)
- Delaware News, "DMV Fees Increase in October 2025" (document fee 4.25% to 5.25%)
- Georgia Department of Revenue, Vehicle Taxes: Title Ad Valorem Tax (7% rate)
- Georgia Department of Revenue, TAVT FAQ (trade-in reduction for dealer sales only)
- Alabama Department of Revenue, "What are the state sales tax rates?" (2% on automotive vehicles)
- Arkansas DFA, Sales Tax Credit for Sale of a Used Vehicle
- Maryland MVA, Excise Tax (6.5% of total purchase price, less a trade-in allowance)
- Minnesota Department of Revenue, Motor Vehicle Sales (6.875% rate, trade-in allowance)
- Mississippi Department of Revenue, Motor Vehicle Licensing FAQs (5% on net price after trade-in; rebates not deducted)
- North Carolina DMV, Vehicle Taxes (3% highway use tax)
- Texas Comptroller, Motor Vehicle Sales and Use Tax (6.25%, standard presumptive value)
- Tennessee Department of Revenue, Automotive Dealership Manual (single-article tax and the $1,600 local cap)
- Washington Department of Revenue, Motor Vehicle Sales/Use Tax (0.5% from January 1, 2026)
- Washington Department of Revenue, Luxury Motor Vehicle Tax (8% over $100,000 from 2026)
- Kentucky Office of State Budget Director, Motor Vehicle Usage Tax report (no trade-in allowance on new vehicles)
- Iowa Code 321.105A (fee for new registration and the same-owner trade-in rule)
- Chicago Auto Show, "New Illinois Law Removes Trade-In Tax Credit Cap" (effective January 1, 2022)
- Oklahoma Policy Institute, Motor Vehicle Taxes (3.25% excise and 1.25% sales tax)

## EV registration fees by state (`ev-registration-fees-by-state-2026.csv`)

- U.S. Department of Energy, Alternative Fuels Data Center, state laws and incentives database (EV, plug-in hybrid and hybrid fee summaries for each state, retrieved October 2026)
- National Conference of State Legislatures, "Special Fees on Plug-In Hybrid and Electric Vehicles" (state fee map, Alabama increase date)
- American Road and Transportation Builders Association, "State Electric Vehicle Fees, April 2026" (42-state table and map)
- Missouri Revised Statutes 142.869, alternative fuel decal fee (via FindLaw)
- Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles, Fee Chart (EV supplemental fee $242, hybrid $81)
- California DMV, Registration Fees (Road Improvement Fee for zero-emission vehicles)
- Georgia Department of Revenue, Alternative Fuel and Low-Speed Vehicles Annual Licensing Fees, July 2026
- Virginia DMV, Highway Use Fee
- Oregon DMV, new fees effective December 31, 2025 (one-page summary)
- Bridge Michigan, "Michigan EV fees spike under road funding deal, now nation's highest" (January 2026, citing the Michigan Secretary of State)
- New Jersey Coalition of Automotive Retailers, "NJMVC electric vehicle fee update" (fee schedule 2024-2028)
- New Mexico Legislature, Senate Bill 2 (2026 regular session, EV fee schedule from 2027)
- Holland and Hart, "House Transportation and Infrastructure Committee Advances $580 Billion BUILD America 250 Act" (May 22, 2026)
- News Tribune, "CBO: House highway bill would widen trust fund" (July 12, 2026; bill status and fee schedule)

## EV home charging cost by state (`ev-home-charging-cost-by-state-2026.csv`)

- U.S. Energy Information Administration, Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A, "Average Price of Electricity to Ultimate Customers by End-Use Sector, by State" (residential, August 2026 preliminary and August 2025)
- U.S. Department of Energy and EPA, fueleconomy.gov, 2025-2026 EV ratings (Model 3 Standard RWD, Leaf 75 kWh, Ioniq 5 RWD, Equinox EV FWD, F-150 Lightning 4WD ER1)
- Alternative Fuels Data Center, "Charging Electric Vehicles at Home" (cost per mile and full-charge examples)
- Alternative Fuels Data Center, "Electric Vehicle Charging Stations" (Level 1 and Level 2 speeds)
- InsideEVs, "How Efficient Is Each Type Of EV Charger?" (March 8, 2024; ADAC loss tests and about 15% average AC loss)
- Eviqo, "EV Charger Installation Cost in 2026" (updated July 2026; installation, hardware, labor, panel upgrade)
- Sunrun, "How Much Does It Cost to Install an EV Charger at Home" (February 2026)
